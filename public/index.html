Our Story 🍯

Teddy & Co was born from a simple dream: to create a space where everyone can feel like a kid again. We specialize in whimsical, delicious treats that are as photogenic as they are tasty!

Every waffle is baked fresh, shaped like our iconic croc shoes, and can be customized with our rich sauces, fresh fruits, and artisanal ice cream.
